# MinArea, cross-fitted

> **Mechanism corrected** by [2026-10-02-minarea-disk](2026-10-02-minarea-disk.md): `421` and
> `217` are fired seeds whose own `P > 0.5` component is ~700 px, not disks. The disk is not
> the constraint on `MinArea`. The numbers below stand.

Brief, prediction and gate: [../briefs/minarea.md](../briefs/minarea.md), committed before any
run.

**Question.** `Post.MinArea = 500` was never measured. It is the size below which relax's
component falls back to the disk and below which a component cannot be claimed. Does another
value do better on the current configuration?

**Protocol.** 68 images (DRAC22 41, TUH 27), cached 5-fold CV `P` through `run_cache`,
whole-image scoring. Current configuration: `tau` 0.5, relax 0.20 at 128 px, boundary
claiming, `hullLink` 128. Grid `MinArea` ∈ {100, 250, 500, 750, 1000}. For each fold, choose
the value with the best mean IoU on the other four folds (ties within 0.001 go to 500, then
the nearest value) and score it on the held-out fold. Paired per image against 500, which
reproduces `2026-09-30-hull-128.csv` to 5e-16. Percentile bootstrap, 10 000 resamples,
`rng(20260917)`, 95 % CI; exact two-sided sign test. Script: a scratch `.m` file, not
committed.

## Result: every fold chooses 500. The gate fails and 500 stays.

| | IoU | median | F1 | precision | recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| default, 500 | 0.5207 | 0.5375 | 0.6572 | 0.6268 | 0.7762 |
| `MinArea` cross-fitted per fold | 0.5207 | 0.5375 | 0.6572 | 0.6268 | 0.7762 |

All five folds choose 500. The cross-fitted masks are therefore the default's, and Δ is
exactly 0 on all 68 images.

| gate | result | |
|---|---|---|
| 1. ΔIoU 95 % CI lower bound > 0 | 0.0000 | fail |
| 2. precision guard, three smallest GT-area quintiles | 0.5291 → 0.5291 | pass |
| 3. ≥ 4 of 5 folds choose the same value | 5 of 5 on 500 | pass, on the default |

### Above 500 is a cliff, not a slope

At 750, two images carry 99 % of the loss. Both are DRAC22 single-seed images with the
domain's two smallest lesions:

| image | seeds | GT px | predicted px at 500 | at 750 | IoU 500 → 750 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `421` | 1 | 674 | 699 | 0 | 0.871 → 0 |
| `217` | 1 | 601 | 708 | 0 | 0.836 → 0 |

Their whole prediction is one ~700 px component, about the disk's area. Above that size it
becomes unclaimable, the seed has nothing else to claim, and the image goes empty. The other
eight changed images move by −0.017 to +0.011. **`MinArea` must stay below the smallest
lesion and below the disk (~707 px).** At 500 it does both, with about 100 px to spare.

### Against the prediction

- **Right:** 100 and 250 are within ±0.002 of 500. They are in fact nearly inert, changing 7
  and 4 images. The folds choose 500, and the gate fails.
- **Wrong in magnitude:** 750 and 1000 were predicted to cost 0.002–0.006. They cost
  0.024–0.025, because a lesion smaller than `MinArea` cannot be output at all, and two
  such images go to IoU 0.
- **Not seen:** the predicted precision gain on Q1–Q2 at 100 and 250. Q1 is unchanged at both.

## Conclusion

500 stays, now measured. Below it the setting is inert on this `P`. Above ~700 px it removes
the smallest lesions outright. It is a floor on the output's resolution, not a tuning knob.
